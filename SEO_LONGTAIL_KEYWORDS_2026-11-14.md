# Long-tail, no-backlink keyword research - 14 November 2026

Goal: keywords veloradigitizing.com (AS 2, ~4 months old, no real links) can reach page 1 for in weeks, on the strength of on-page content alone.

Inputs: SEO_SERP_REVIEW_2026-11-09.md (L1-L62, cluster verdicts, 12 Nov Semrush bulk table) and the 62 existing posts (slug, metaTitle, tags). Keywords already covered there were not re-researched.

## Method and limits

- 61 SERP checks by web search (US engine, signed out). Results are an approximate page 1, not a rank-exact Google pull. Treat "who ranks" as composition evidence (forums, small blogs, small digitizers versus brands, marketplaces and tools), not as exact positions.
- **Rankable without backlinks** means page 1 contains at least 2 of: forums/UGC (Reddit, Quora, embroideres.com forum, Google Groups, hackmd/dev.to, blogspot, Medium), small blogs, thin or pre-2024 pages, or posts from small digitizing companies (ZDigitizing, EmbPunch, Eagle Digitizing, DigitEMB, TrueDigitizing, MIG Digitizing, Absolute Digitizing), and no wall of big brands, marketplaces or tools.
- Rejected on intent: tool/converter, shopping/product grid, design/font download, navigational.
- No volume or KD data was pulled in this round (no Semrush access from here). **Owner action:** run the "Do now" and "Absorb" keywords through Semrush bulk check before writing, and drop anything with 0 volume and no close variant above 20.
- Difficulty labels: **Easy** = mostly forums, help docs, thin vendor posts and small digitizers; **Medium** = small sites present but mixed with one or two strong brands or POD platforms (Printful, Custom Ink, Rush Order Tees); **Hard** = brand, marketplace or product wall.
