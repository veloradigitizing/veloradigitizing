**A "real" referring domain** is one that passes all four tests:

1. A human embroiderer, apparel decorator or print shop owner might plausibly visit the page.
2. The link sits in editorial content, a profile the owner controls, or a listing with human review.
3. The domain is not a .shop / .xyz / .online / .site / .click network domain, not a "link juice directory", not a page that exists only to sell SEO.
4. Nobody was paid for the link itself (paying for a platform subscription such as Etsy fees is fine).

Nofollow links from real places (Reddit, Pinterest, Etsy, YouTube) count toward the target. They do not pass authority the way follow links do, but they bring real visitors, brand searches and discovery, which is what a four-month-old domain lacks most. Aim for a natural mix; do not reject a source because it is nofollow.

### What not to do, and why

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy "DA 50+ backlinks", PBN links, niche edits or guest post packages on Fiverr, Upwork, Legiit or Facebook groups | The current 92 spam domains are exactly what those deliver. Google ignores them at best; at worst they are the pattern that triggers a manual action on a new domain. |
| Mass-submit to free directories that accept anything | They look like the domraider listings already in the profile. Zero traffic, zero trust. |
| Post the same promotional paragraph in ten Reddit threads | Account bans, and the subreddits that rank for our keywords are moderated by working embroiderers who recognise it instantly. |
| Create fake reviews, fake testimonials or fake client counts | The RoleFate link exists because an article was genuinely useful. Fabrication is the opposite of that, and it is checkable. |
| Build citations with the Los Angeles address before it is confirmed real | A citation profile that Google cannot match to a verifiable business is worse than no citations. See section 8. |
| Chase .edu/.gov links through scholarship or "resource page" spam | Off-topic, widely abused, and not how the competitors got their authority. |

### Reddit and Facebook participation rules

- Weeks 1 to 3: answer questions with no links at all. Density, underlay, pull compensation, cap sequencing, foam thickness, stabilizer by fabric. Be specific with numbers.
- From week 4: link to a Velora guide only when someone asks for a resource, or when the answer genuinely needs the chart or calculator. One link per answer, never to a service page, never in the first sentence.
- Never post "we offer digitizing" or respond to "who should I use" threads with Velora. Those threads rank for "best embroidery digitizing service"; a genuine recommendation from another member is the only thing that works there, and self-recommendation gets removed.
- Keep the same username across Reddit, Facebook and Quora so the name becomes recognisable.
- Target: 4 to 5 useful answers per week, not more.

> The foam poking out at the ends is almost always missing end caps, not density. On a 2 mm foam you want the satin columns capped with a perpendicular run at each open end so the needle cuts the foam cleanly, then density around 0.18 to 0.20 mm spacing with no zigzag underlay underneath the puff (a centre run only). ## 8. Google Business Profile and citations: only with a real address

The local pack appeared on nearly every commercial SERP reviewed on 9 November (Crystal Digitizing Service ranks in it with one review). A verified GBP would be the single strongest missing signal for the service keywords. It is also the one thing that can do lasting damage if faked.

**Do this only if the address at `128 Business Blvd, Suite 204, Los Angeles, CA 90017` (from the off-page kit) is a real location where someone can receive Google's verification postcard or complete video verification.** If it is a virtual office or a made-up address: skip GBP and all citation directories. Google suspends profiles at addresses it cannot verify, suspensions are hard to lift, and a NAP spread across directories that does not match any verifiable business lowers trust in the domain rather than raising it. No local pack is better than a suspended one.

If the address is real, requirements in order:

1. Exactly one NAP everywhere: name, address, phone as in kit section 1; the same on the site footer, schema, Etsy, Fiverr, LinkedIn and every directory.
2. Primary category: Embroidery service. Secondary: Graphic designer, Custom t-shirt store only if physically true.
3. Services listed individually: cap logo digitizing, 3D puff digitizing, applique digitizing, patch digitizing, vector art conversion, with the price-from values matching /pricing.
4. Photos: real workstation, real sew-outs, real machine if there is one; no stock images.
5. First five reviews from real clients who agree to be asked, spaced over weeks, never in one day, never incentivised. Reply to every review.
6. Then, and only then, run kit section 2 directories with the identical NAP.

If the business is actually run from Pakistan, the honest alternative is a service-area or online-only presentation with the real country, which cannot use the US local pack but also cannot be suspended for it.
